Privacy Policy for rclone-servers

Last updated: October 26, 2026

rclone-servers is a personal tool used only by its owner to access the owner's own Google Drive through the rclone command-line program.

Data accessed

The app accesses only the owner's own Google Drive files, and only when the owner runs rclone to upload, download or list them.

Data collection and storage

The app has no server and no database. It does not collect, store, sell, share or transmit any user data to any third party. OAuth tokens are stored only in rclone's configuration files on the owner's own machines.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
